# Research

Owner: Engine, with HMI research prototypes linked from `docs/team-guides/hmi`.

This folder is for curated research summaries, literature reviews and final benchmarking reports. Working notebooks and generated experiment output should remain in prototypes, tutorials or external model/data storage until promoted.

Recommended grouping:

- `literature/` for papers and literature review summaries
- `model-evaluation/` for final model comparison reports
- `data-sources/` for data source reviews
- `simulation/` for simulator research

Generated MLflow runs, caches and model weights do not belong here.

HMI-specific research prototypes should stay under `src/prototypes/hmi/research` until the HMI team decides whether they belong in docs, production assets or archive.
